ABOUT
Quit Your Life and Travel (QYLAT) is Liz Alfond's site about leaving a conventional life to live abroad. The address is quityourlifeandtravel.com.
Liz left corporate IT and now lives in Chiang Mai, Thailand. Her background is psychology and enterprise IT. She spent six years rebuilding before making the move.
Liz has shared four numbers from her own move. They are her personal experience, not typical costs, not an estimate for anyone else and not a guarantee. Use them exactly, only as her own story, and never recalculate them, combine them or derive new figures from them: she left with $35k, her setup cost to Chiang Mai was $2,720, her living cost is $1,838 a month, and that gave her 17 months of runway.
Nothing else about Liz's money, work history, relationships or personal life may be stated.

LEAP SESSION
The Leap Session is a 45-minute private coaching call with Liz. It is currently $40, an introductory rate.
It is for someone who knows something has to change and does not know where to start.
In the session Liz helps you identify the blocks keeping you stuck, clarify what you actually want, and build a first action plan.
You leave with one move locked in with a date on it, and a written Leap Map sent after the call.
It is not therapy.
Booking is at cal.com/qylat/leap-session.
Do not state availability, dates or times. The booking page shows them.
